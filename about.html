About Us

The Commonwealth Police Sports Union (CPU) is the organisation responsible for the development of sports among Police Forces of the Commonwealth and the organisation of the Commonwealth Police Games. It is an organisation headquartered and incorporated in India.
Working President: Vinod Kumar Mall, IPS, Former Director General of Police of the State of Gujarat, India
Founder Secretary: Sunil Kumar, Secretary of Police & Fire Games Board of India
