Our Mission

The mission of the CPU is to promote sports among the police forces in Commonwealth Countries and to coordinate the preparation, organization, and participation of police athletes from Commonwealth Countries in international competitions across all sports disciplines. This includes building social, cooperative, and professional relations between police forces, contributing to the investigation of sports sciences, promoting human solidarity and cultural exchanges, organizing sport-related seminars and scientific courses, and fostering strong ties among police officers in Commonwealth Countries.
